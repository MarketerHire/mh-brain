# mh-brain

A growth operating system that runs on your brand's actual data — not generic best practices.

---

**How it works:**

![mh-brain loop](./mh-brain-loop.excalidraw.png)

Your brand goes in — products, customers, voice, competitive positioning. From there, mh-brain executes real marketing work: emails, ad creative, landing pages, SEO content, retention flows. Every piece of output ties directly to a revenue lever — traffic, conversion, repeat purchase, lifetime value.

When results come back, the system doesn't just report them. It studies what worked, what didn't, and why. Those patterns feed the next round of execution. The emails get sharper. The ad hooks land harder. The content targets the right segments. Not because someone guessed — because the system saw what actually moved the number.

**What you get, week to week:**

Campaigns that ship on time. Content that sounds like your brand, not a template. Performance insights that connect spend to revenue. And a system that remembers every result, so the same mistake never happens twice and the best ideas get reused automatically.

**What "smarter over time" actually means:**

On day one, mh-brain knows your brand guidelines and your data. By day 30, it knows which hooks your customers respond to, which segments convert, and which channels are underperforming. By day 90, it has built a working model of your business — seasonality, price sensitivity, competitive dynamics — drawn from thousands of real data points. That model sharpens every week.

**Getting started takes one conversation.** We need your brand positioning, your customer profiles, your product catalog, and access to your platforms (Shopify, Klaviyo, ad accounts). Most brands have this ready in a couple of hours.

**What makes this different from an agency or ChatGPT:**

An agency restarts from scratch every engagement. ChatGPT gives you generic answers with no memory of what you tried last week. mh-brain compounds. Every execution builds on the last one. After 90 days, it carries institutional knowledge about your brand that would take a new hire months to develop — and it never forgets, never leaves, and never stops learning.

**The moat is simple:** the longer you run it, the more it knows, and the harder it is to replace with anything that doesn't already have that knowledge.
